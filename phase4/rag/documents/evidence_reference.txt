DOCUMENT TITLE: Evidence Interpretation Reference

PURPOSE:
Demonstration reference material for interpreting structured evidence
generated by the Insurance-Fraud-AI system.

EVIDENCE TYPES:

DOCUMENT EVIDENCE:
OCR and document classification can identify the type and contents
of submitted claim documents.

FINANCIAL EVIDENCE:
Invoice totals, repair amounts, and calculated differences can be
used to identify financial inconsistencies.

SIGNATURE EVIDENCE:
Signature similarity and signature matching results can be used as
supporting evidence when evaluating document consistency.

VEHICLE EVIDENCE:
Traditional image analysis and ML-based vehicle damage classification
can provide separate damage signals.

MODEL EVIDENCE:
Fraud probability represents the output of a trained fraud
classification model.

IMPORTANT:
No individual signal should automatically be interpreted as proof
of fraudulent activity.

CONFIDENCE:
Low-confidence ML predictions should be treated cautiously and may
require additional investigation.

CONTRADICTIONS:
When two evidence sources disagree, the disagreement itself can be
an investigation signal.

END OF DOCUMENT.
