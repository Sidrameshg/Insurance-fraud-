DOCUMENT TITLE: Insurance Claim Investigation Reference

PURPOSE:
This document is a demonstration knowledge-base document for the
Insurance-Fraud-AI Phase 4 RAG system.

INVESTIGATION PRINCIPLES:

1. A fraud model prediction should be treated as a risk signal,
   not as definitive proof of fraud.

2. Investigators should review multiple evidence sources before
   making a final decision.

3. Differences between claimed repair amounts and invoice amounts
   should be investigated when the difference is significant.

4. Vehicle damage predictions should be interpreted together with
   other claim evidence.

5. Conflicting evidence from different analysis methods should be
   flagged for human review.

6. Documents belonging to the same claim should be compared for
   consistency.

7. Investigation explanations should identify the evidence supporting
   a conclusion and should clearly state uncertainty.

8. The investigation system should not invent facts that are absent
   from the supplied claim evidence.

REVIEW PRINCIPLE:

A claim should be escalated for investigation when multiple
independent evidence signals indicate inconsistency or elevated risk.

END OF DOCUMENT.
